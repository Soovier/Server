**Date:** {date}

**Seller:** {sellerName}, as Personal Representative of the Estate of {decedent}

2. **Earnest money.** {earnestMoney} deposited with {titleCompany} within 3 business days of acceptance.
3. **Condition.** Buyer accepts the property as-is. Seller makes no repairs.
4. **Inspection period.** Buyer may inspect and may cancel for any reason within {inspectionDays} days of acceptance, with earnest money returned.
5. **Closing.** On or before {closingDate}, or within 10 days after any required court approval, at {titleCompany}.

7. **Assignment.** Buyer may assign this agreement. Seller acknowledges Buyer may receive a fee for the assignment.
8. **Personal property.** Items left in the house after closing become Buyer's property.
9. **Closing costs.** Buyer pays customary buyer and seller closing costs, except Seller pays any liens and estate debts.
